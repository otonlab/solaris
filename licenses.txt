  (https://www.cosmos.esa.int/gaia), processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium
  (DPAC, https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dpac/consortium). Funding for the DPAC has been provided
  by national institutions, in particular the institutions participating in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement.
- Distances: Bailer-Jones, C.A.L. et al. 2021, AJ 161, 147 (ESA Gaia Archive, CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO).
- Hipparcos new reduction: van Leeuwen 2007, A&A 474, 653. Credit: ESA
  (via CDS/VizieR I/311 and the ESA Gaia Archive; ESA archive data: CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO).
- SIMBAD: This research has made use of the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France
  (Wenger et al. 2000, A&AS 143, 9). ODbL.
- VizieR: This research has made use of the VizieR catalogue access tool, CDS, Strasbourg, France
  (DOI: 10.26093/cds/vizier).
- 2MASS Redshift Survey: Huchra et al. 2012, ApJS 199, 26 (CDS/VizieR J/ApJS/199/26).
- Cosmicflows-4: Tully et al. 2023, ApJ 944, 94 (CDS/VizieR J/ApJ/944/94);
  velocity field: Courtois et al. 2023, A&A 670, L15. Laniakea basin computed by SOLARIS (estimate).
- Local Group: McConnachie 2012, AJ 144, 4 (CDS/VizieR J/AJ/144/4).
- SDSS DR17: Abdurro'uf et al. 2022, ApJS 259, 35. Funding for the Sloan Digital Sky Survey IV has been
  provided by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science, and the
  Participating Institutions. (SDSS public data: public domain)
- SOLARIS Stellar Catalog (optional star table, ?stars=ssc): stars cross-matched and selected by SOLARIS
  from Gaia DR3 (ESA/Gaia/DPAC, CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO), Bailer-Jones et al. 2021 distances,
  Hipparcos new reduction (ESA) and SDSS DR17 APOGEE effective temperatures
  (Majewski et al. 2017, AJ 154, 94; SDSS public data: public domain).
  Colour-temperature relations: Mucciarelli & Bellazzini 2020, RNAAS 4, 52; Ballesteros 2012, EPL 97, 34008.
  Cross-match check: Brandt 2021, ApJS 254, 42 (Hipparcos-Gaia Catalog of Accelerations, CDS/VizieR).
- Cosmological parameters: Planck Collaboration 2020, A&A 641, A6.
- Narration: AivisSpeech (Aivis Project). Voices: 阿井田 茂 (CV/model: 古山キリヲ),
  morioki (voice: もりおき / model: yuki). Aivis Common Model License (ACML) 1.0.
  morioki は実在の方の声をもとにした合成音声で、ご本人が話したものではありません。
- Tour music: synthesized by SOLARIS (no external audio).

[暦（惑星・月・彗星の位置）]
- Ephemerides: JPL Horizons On-Line Ephemeris System, NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics Group
  (Giorgini, J.D. and JPL Solar System Dynamics Group; https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/).
  Planetary ephemeris DE441 (Park et al. 2021, AJ 161, 105). Positions were fitted with Chebyshev
  polynomials by SOLARIS. Includes the 1981-2000 and 2050-2065 era packs.

[彗星・恒星間天体]
- Orbits of 1P/Halley, 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko, C/1995 O1 (Hale-Bopp), 1I/'Oumuamua, 2I/Borisov
  and 3I/ATLAS (C/2025 N1): JPL Horizons and the JPL Small-Body Database
  (NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics Group; https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/tools/sbdb_lookup.html).
- Event dates and observed properties: ESA, NASA, Minor Planet Center (MPECs) and the published papers
  listed on each object's "出典" screen (e.g. Micheli et al. 2018, Nature 559, 223; Jewitt et al. 2025,
  ApJL 990, L2). Only numbers and facts are used; no observatory images are included.
- Nucleus shapes, comae, tails and the 3I/ATLAS "observation recreation" are computed and drawn by
  SOLARIS (estimates / visualization), not observational images.
- "もしも宇宙船だったら？" spaceships (1I「黒曜の双刃」, 3I「三重環の天体儀」): original fictional 3D models
  made by SOLARIS with Blender. They are not based on any real spacecraft and are not observations.
  No third-party models, textures, HDRIs or fonts are used.

[Apollo の旅・スペースシャトル]
- Launch sounds: NASA (Apollo 10 liftoff, "Remembering Apollo 11" CD-ROM, NASA History Office 1999;
  STS-131 "Sound of Launch", NASA Historical Sounds). Filtered and looped by SOLARIS.
  The Space Shuttle STS-1 launch uses these recordings as a stand-in (not the STS-1 recording).
- Lunar images: JAXA/SELENE (Kaguya) Terrain Camera (ISAS/JAXA), via USGS Astrogeology; modified by SOLARIS.
- Lunar terrain: LROC NAC DTM (NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University); SLDEM2015 (NASA/GSFC, JAXA; Barker et al. 2016).
- Kennedy Space Center imagery: USDA, USGS The National Map: Orthoimagery (USDA NAIP ほか).
- Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025–2026.
- Elevation: USGS 3DEP.
