  and 3I/ATLAS (C/2025 N1): JPL Horizons and the JPL Small-Body Database

  No third-party models, textures, HDRIs or fonts are used.

[⚫ いて座A*（銀河の中心のブラックホール）]
  Mass 4.297 × 10^6 M☉ and distance 8,277 pc: GRAVITY Collaboration 2022 (A&A 657, L12); the galactic frame of SOLARIS uses 8,275 pc (GRAVITY Collaboration 2021, A&A 647, A59).
  Ring diameter 51.8 ± 2.3 μas: Event Horizon Telescope Collaboration 2022 (ApJL 930, L12); model comparison (spin, inclination):
  EHT Sgr A* Paper V (ApJL 930, L16). Hot spots near the ISCO: GRAVITY Collaboration 2018 (A&A 618, L10); Wielgus et al. 2022 (A&A 665, L6).
  Light paths: Schwarzschild geodesics computed in this app; shadow edge Synge 1966 (MNRAS 131, 463).
  Spinning black holes (Kerr): geodesics in Mino time (Mino 2003, Phys. Rev. D 67, 084027), computed in this app; ISCO Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky 1972 (ApJ 178, 347);
  shadow edge Bardeen 1973 (in Black Holes, Les Houches 1972, eds. DeWitt & DeWitt). Spin values 0.5 and 0.94 are assumptions (models compared in EHT Paper V).
  Accretion flow (model): radiatively inefficient flow after Broderick & Loeb 2006 (MNRAS 367, 905) and Yuan et al. 2003 (ApJ 598, 301), simplified.
  S-stars: orbital elements from Gillessen et al. 2017 (ApJ 837, 30), Table 3 (S111 omitted). K-band extinction A_K = 2.46: Schödel et al. 2010 (A&A 511, A18).
  Nuclear star cluster (estimated model, not real star positions): density slopes after Schödel et al. 2014 (A&A 566, A47) and 2018 (A&A 609, A27);
  young massive stars after Paumard et al. 2006 (ApJ 643, 1011) and Lu et al. 2009 (ApJ 690, 1463).
  Minispiral (Sgr A West): Keplerian orbits of the three streams, Zhao, Morris, Goss & An 2009 (ApJ 699, 186), Table 5. Circumnuclear disk (estimated model): Jackson et al. 1993 (ApJ 402, 173).
  Reference for the film comparison: James, von Tunzelmann, Franklin & Thorne 2015 (Class. Quantum Grav. 32, 065001).

[⚫ ブラックホールの一覧（M87*・はくちょう座X-1・Gaia BH1・Gaia BH3）]
  M87*: mass 6.5 × 10^9 M☉ and distance 16.8 Mpc, EHT Collaboration 2019 (ApJL 875, L6); jet inclination 17° and position angle 288°, Walker et al. 2018 (ApJ 855, 128).
  Cygnus X-1: mass 21.2 M☉, distance 2.22 kpc, orbital inclination 27.51°, companion HDE 226868 (22.3 R☉, 31,138 K, separation 0.244 au) and spin a > 0.9985,
  Miller-Jones et al. 2021 (Science 371, 1046; spin analysis after Zhao et al. 2021, ApJ 908, 117). The companion's position along its orbit is not modeled (placeholder).
  Thin accretion disk: Novikov & Thorne 1973; Page & Thorne 1974 (ApJ 191, 499) — flux computed in this app (peak at r = 9.55 GM/c² checked).
  Gaia BH1: El-Badry et al. 2023 (MNRAS 518, 1057); mass 9.27 M☉, Nagarajan et al. 2024 (PASP 136, 014202). Gaia BH3: Gaia Collaboration, Panuzzo et al. 2024 (A&A 686, L2).
